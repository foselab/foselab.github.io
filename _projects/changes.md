The PE CHANGES project is funded by the European Union through NextGenerationEU under Italy's National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR), Mission 4, Component 2, Investment 1.3.

CHANGES – Cultural Heritage Active Innovation for Sustainable Society aims to support teaching and scientific and technological research in cultural heritage, culture, and the protection of historical and cultural heritage.
