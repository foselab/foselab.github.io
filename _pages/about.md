Complex software should be understandable, testable, and dependable. At FOSELAB, the Formal Methods & Software Engineering group at the [University of Bergamo](https://www.unibg.it/en), we bring rigorous methods into the design and development of systems that must behave reliably.

We use **model-driven engineering**, software architecture, UML, and **Abstract State Machines** to describe systems precisely. With the **ASMETA** toolset, simulation, scenario-based validation, model checking, and runtime monitoring, we examine how designs behave, how components interact, and whether key requirements continue to hold.

We also develop **model-based**, **combinatorial**, and **search-based testing** techniques to find failures and keep tests useful as software evolves. We apply these methods to the safety of cyber-physical, autonomous, and self-adaptive systems, including drones, vehicles, and medical devices. We explore how language models can assist formal specification and how to validate synthetic data when real-world data is limited.
